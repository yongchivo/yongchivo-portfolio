PHV Prep UK is now live on both the App Store and Google Play. It's an exam
preparation app for the Wolverhampton Private Hire Vehicle licensing test — the
knowledge test UK drivers sit to get their taxi badge — and it's built around a
single idea: practise until the key questions are automatic.

## What's in the app

- **300+ exam-style questions** across the seven official categories.
- **Timed mock exams** — 28 questions, 45 minutes, under real exam conditions.
- **The 8 key questions rule**, marked the way the real test marks it: get even
  one key question wrong and you fail the whole paper, no matter how good your
  overall score is.
- **An AI Tutor** — ask anything about the exam and get an instant answer in
  plain English.
- **Progress tracking by category**, so you can see where you're weak and drill
  exactly that.

## Why both platforms matter

96% of Wolverhampton-licensed PHV drivers live outside Wolverhampton. Because of
cross-border licensing, this was never a local audience — it's a national one.

A large share of those drivers use Android. Staying iOS-only meant missing many
of the people the app was built for.

It's the same app on both platforms: same content, same features, no
second-class version.

## Built solo

I'm a solo developer and a Cyber Security student at the University of
Worcester. PHV Prep UK is built with Flutter and Firebase, and the AI Tutor runs
on Anthropic's Claude API, called server-side through a Firebase Cloud Function
so the key never ships inside the app.

Getting onto two app stores wasn't smooth. Google rejected the Android build
twice before approval — including a Misleading Claims policy violation, which
required listing all seven official source documents with verified URLs and
adding a visible non-affiliation disclaimer.
